# TTI Consent-Aware Phone Team Activation Register

**Reference date:** 2026-08-12 Pacific Time  
**Status:** **Not active.** The current session has no authorized telephony provider. A disabled documentation-only Twilio reference exists; it cannot place or receive calls.

## Purpose

This register stages the full phone-team layer after the rapid operations architecture. It does not authorize calls, text messages, recording, caller-ID changes, surveillance, interception, or outreach to unverified contact lists.

## Required Activation Controls

| Activation control | Required evidence before activation | Current state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authorized carrier/provider | An enabled telephony connection with the user’s verified credentials and granted calling permissions. | Not connected. |
| Authorized business number | Verified ownership/control of the exact outbound/inbound number. | Not verified in the telephony system. |
| Identity disclosure | Approved introduction naming Total Transformation Inc. and the purpose of the call. | Draft required. |
| Contact and consent source | A documented, lawful source of recipients and any applicable consent/relationship basis. | Not established. |
| Do-not-call and opt-out rules | Suppression list, opt-out capture, and immediate enforcement process. | Not established. |
| Calling hours and geography rules | Calling-window and location policy based on recipient location and applicable requirements. | Not established. |
| Recording and privacy policy | Clear recording-consent handling, retention limits, and restricted data policy. | Not established. |
| Escalation owner | Named process for complaints, threats, sensitive disclosures, sales disputes, and legal/medical/financial questions. | Staged in rapid-team governance roles. |
| Script and claim review | Evidence-based scripts with no deception, impersonation, guarantees, regulated advice, or pressure. | Draft required. |
| Authorized test | A controlled test to an authorized number with logging and no real prospect solicitation. | Not possible until provider and number are ready. |

## Allowed Initial Phone Roles After Activation

| Role | Allowed function | Required boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inbound receptionist | Identify the business, route a caller, capture a stated request. | No sensitive-data collection beyond the stated request. |
| Appointment coordinator | Offer authorized scheduling options to a consented/returning contact. | No pressure, deception, or unauthorized calendar access. |
| Existing inquiry follow-up | Respond to a person who directly requested contact. | Use documented consent/relationship and opt-out process. |
| Customer support triage | Route factual product-delivery or support issues. | No financial, legal, medical, tax, credit, or clinical advice. |
| Funder/partner callback router | Return a requested call from a verified agency/funder contact. | Follow the requester’s stated process; no grant or eligibility claim. |

## Excluded Functions

The phone team may not conduct surveillance, use or emulate Stingray/cell-site interception, obtain location/device data, spoof a caller identity, place unsolicited robocalls, harass recipients, avoid opt-out requests, impersonate a person or institution, collect payment credentials, offer regulated advice, or claim that funding, sales, contracts, licensing, partnerships, or service outcomes are guaranteed.

## First Authorized Test Sequence

1. Connect and verify the telephony provider and number.
2. Load the approved identity disclosure, opt-out sentence, escalation contacts, and test script.
3. Call an authorized test recipient only.
4. Confirm caller identity, logging, routing, recording/consent behavior, and opt-out handling.
5. Record the outcome in the execution ledger.
6. Permit only the limited roles above until observed results justify a documented expansion.

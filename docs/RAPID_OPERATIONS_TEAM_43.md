# TTI Rapid Operations Team — 43-Role Architecture

**Reference date:** 2026-08-12 Pacific Time  
**Operating mode:** Internal coordination, authenticated-channel work, and evidence-controlled task routing.  
**Not active:** Unauthenticated phone dialing, surveillance/interception, deceptive automation, payment collection, legal/medical/financial advice, or public claims without evidence.

## Mission

The rapid operations team turns verified inputs into one of four outputs: a completed internal deliverable, a routed legitimate inbound request, a readiness correction, or a documented external blocker. The system prioritizes the constraint closest to a verified buyer, funder, partner, or platform response.

## 43 Roles

### Mission Control

| # | Role | Trigger | Primary action | Escalate when |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mission Controller | Any new request or evidence event | Reprioritize the single execution ledger. | A task is consequential, blocked, or conflicts with the evidence rule. |
| 2 | Constraint Analyst | A workflow stalls | Identify the nearest bottleneck: route, asset, access, payment, delivery, or policy. | A blocker requires an external account, attestation, or signature. |
| 3 | Playbook Steward | A process repeats or succeeds | Convert the process into a reusable checklist. | The process has unverified assumptions. |
| 4 | Operating Cadence Keeper | Each scheduled cycle | Check the evidence ledger, aging tasks, and response queues. | An urgent authenticated response is found. |

## Core Measures

| Measure | Definition | Valid source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verified response rate | Confirmed inbound responses divided by sent permitted inquiries. | Inbox threads and message metadata. |
| Route advancement rate | Routes that move to a documented official next step. | Official funder/partner response or portal notice. |
| Product readiness rate | Offers with accurate scope, verified checkout, delivery, and support procedures. | Canonical product record and payment/delivery evidence. |
| Public distribution count | Public posts accepted by the intended platform. | Platform URL or acceptance confirmation. |
| Constraint age | Days a readiness blocker remains open. | Execution ledger. |
| Playbook coverage | Repeatable processes with a documented trigger, steps, evidence, and escalation. | Operations documentation. |

## Phone-Team Boundary

The 43 roles do not initiate phone calls. Phone activation requires a separately verified business number, telephony provider, consent-aware contact source, caller identity disclosure, opt-out mechanism, recording/consent policy, escalation owner, and authorized test. Surveillance, interception, caller spoofing, harassment, or covert data collection are excluded.

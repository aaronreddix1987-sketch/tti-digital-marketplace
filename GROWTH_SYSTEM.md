# TTI Growth System

This repository is the marketplace planning surface for Total Transformation Inc. The growth system is **evidence-first**: a product, service, checkout path, automation, or campaign is not described as live until the relevant platform, payment, delivery, and outcome evidence exists.

## Operating Sequence

1. Maintain one canonical product/service record with scope, source asset, payment method, delivery route, owner, and support path.
2. Validate one buyer problem and one offer at a time before expanding distribution.
3. Publish only through authenticated channels using real product assets and accurately scoped language.
4. Log product posts, inbound interest, qualifications, payments, deliveries, support issues, and refunds as discrete events.
5. Turn repeatable successful paths into playbooks; resolve the highest-impact constraint before adding complexity.

## Current Gates

| Gate | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Product readiness | Current product version, accurate scope, and delivery asset. |
| Checkout readiness | Current verified payment route mapped to the exact offer. |
| Fulfillment readiness | Delivery procedure and support owner. |
| Channel readiness | Authenticated account and successful platform action. |
| Service readiness | Scoped workflow, approval boundaries, delivery capacity, and quality-control path. |

## Safety Boundary

Do not publish unverified revenue, client, margin, guarantee, credit, tax, legal, medical, recovery, housing, or automation-performance claims. Do not process payments, configure payouts, collect credentials, or make regulated commitments from this repository.

## Measurement

The source-of-truth metrics are verified-ready SKU rate, public product posts, qualified inbound rate, verified checkout conversion, on-time delivery rate, issue/refund rate, time to scoped delivery, playbook coverage, and bottleneck age. Use observed events rather than assumed outcomes.
